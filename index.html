विद्यारम्भं करिष्यामि सिद्धिर्भवतु मे सदा ॥

Pooja Guidelines & Vratam

Gentle reminders and traditional observances for the three-day study offering and Vidyarambham.

🌿

1. Shuddhi (Purity & Preparation)

Inner & Outer Cleanliness

Before offering your books or files, clean your physical study space and dust your notebooks. Wash your hands or take a bath, light an incense stick, and take a moment of quiet reflection to clear your mind of stress and clutter.

🪔

2. Samarpanam (Reverent Offering)

Pooja Vaypu Rites

On Durgashtami evening, offer your textbooks, primary study notes, and research materials to the altar. Set a clear intention for the learning ahead. Remember that knowledge (Vidya) is sacred and worthy of humble gratitude.

🧘

3. Upavasam (The Study Pause)

Contemplative Rest

Throughout Mahanavami, refrain from secular study, casual browsing, or stressful preparation. Treat this period as a mindful study fast—listen to classical ragas, chant the Saraswati slokas, and reflect on past teachers and mentors.

🌸

4. Punar-Arambham (New Dawn)

Vijayadashami Reopening

On Vijayadashami morning after sunrise (Pooja Eduppu), retrieve your study documents. Read a fresh chapter or write the sacred alphabets with renewed humility, focus, and devotion.
