# Vidyadeepam
🪔 VidyaDeepam (വിദ്യാദീപം) — A digital altar honoring Saraswati Pooja &amp; Pooja Vaypu. Reverently pause your studies, light the temple Nilavilakku, and seal your learning materials until Vidyarambham dawn.

## The Inspiration:
In an increasingly digital world, students who study from PDFs, eBooks, and digital notes miss out on the sacred tradition of Pooja Vaypu (പൂജവെയ്പ്പ്) because they cannot physically place their learning materials at the altar. VidyaDeepam was created to bridge this gap—providing a dedicated, mindful virtual sanctuary where modern digital learners can offer, seal, and bless their digital study materials for Navaratri.

### Why VidyaDeepam?
Created for students who rely on digital notes, PDFs, and devices and cannot physically place them for Pooja Vaypu, allowing them to participate in this sacred tradition through a mindful virtual offering.

> **A Sacred Digital Sanctuary for Navaratri, Pooja Vaypu (പൂജവെയ്പ്പ്), and Vidyarambham (വിദ്യാരംഭം)**

## 📖 Overview

**VidyaDeepam** is a peaceful, authentic web application designed around the sacred Kerala tradition of **Pooja Vaypu (പൂജവെയ്പ്പ്)** and **Vidyarambham (വിദ്യാരംഭം)**. 

During the festival of Navaratri, learners, artists, and scholars place their books, notebooks, and tools of trade before Goddess Saraswati on Durgashtami evening. They observe a mindful pause from secular study throughout Mahanavami, before reopening their books on Vijayadashami dawn to resume learning with renewed clarity, humility, and purpose.

VidyaDeepam recreates this reverent temple sanctum digitally—offering an interactive **Kerala Nilavilakku altar**, **organic rising incense smoke**, an integrated **Indian classical raga synthesizer**, and a **client-side study-document vault**.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🪔 Sacred Kerala Temple Altar
- **Interactive Brass Nilavilakku:** High-fidelity tiered Kerala lamp with five wicks featuring procedural, realistic flickering flame animations and warm ground light pools.
- **Burning Agarbathi & Organic Smoke:** Procedural SVG smoke strands that curl, drift softly from side to side, and fade organically above glowing embers.
- **The Lighting Ritual:** Pressing **"Light the Lamp"** rings the sacred temple bell, ignites the five lamp wicks and secondary clay diyas, activates the incense embers, brightens the sanctum aura, and initiates classical temple music.

### 2. 🎶 Indian Classical Temple Music Sanctuary
Powered entirely by the native **Web Audio API** with **zero external audio dependencies or broken links**:
- **Saraswati Veena (*Raga Saraswati*):** Harmonic plucked resonance with subtle *gamaka* oscillations.
- **Temple Bamboo Flute / Bansuri (*Raga Ahir Bhairav*):** Warm, breath-infused morning notes with acoustic vibrato (~5.5 Hz).
- **Classical Sitar (*Raga Bhupali*):** Pentatonic melodic phrases featuring authentic *jawari* bridge buzzing overtones.
- **Sanctum Tanpura & Ghanta Drone:** Continuous, meditative *Pa–Sa–Sa–Kharaj Sa* drone paired with real-time inharmonic temple bell chimes (`🔔`).

### 3. 📚 Private Study Offering Vault (Pooja Box)
- **Offline Client-Side Vault:** Uses the browser's native **IndexedDB** to store uploaded study documents (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, TXT) safely on your local device.
- **Ritual Countdown:** Keeps materials sealed until your selected Vijayadashami opening time.
- **Emergency Early Access:** Allows opening files early if an urgent study deadline arises.
- **Zero Cloud Uploads:** Respects your privacy; your personal documents never leave your browser.

### 4. 🔱 Devi Darshan & Temple Color Palette
- Dedicated shrines and attributes for the divine triad:
  - **Maha Durga:** Saffron-Orange (*Keshara*) — Courage, cleansing of inertia, and removing obstacles.
  - **Maha Lakshmi:** Turmeric-Yellow (*Haridra*) — Auspiciousness, patience, and noble prosperity.
  - **Maha Saraswati:** Tulsi-Green (*Tulsi*) — Purity, memory, speech, and supreme *Vidya*.
